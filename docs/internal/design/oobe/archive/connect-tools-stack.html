Connect-tools · collapse / expand

Archived record of the collapse / expand control for the cold-start "Connect your tools" panel, captured before it was retired from mockup.html. Two states: the expanded pills (each with a checkbox state + a collapse control) and the collapsed overlapping icon stack (with an expand chevron). Built on the real --v2-* tokens; renders in light and dark.

Connect your tools3 connected — tap to manage

Interaction (as designed). Connecting a tool checked its pill; once all were connected the pills collapsed into the overlapping icon stack ("N connected — tap to manage"). Tapping the stack (or its › chevron) re-expanded to the pills; the expanded row carried a ‹ collapse control to fold them back. Retired in favor of a dismiss-to-composer-pill pattern — this file preserves the stack design for reference.
